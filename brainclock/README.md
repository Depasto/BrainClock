# 🧠 BrainClock

> **Your personal day synchronizer.** Take control of your time, plan your tasks, and build custom notifications tailored to your natural daily rhythm.

---

## ✨ About The Project

**BrainClock** is a modern custom clock app built with **Dart**. Designed for those who need more than just a standard alarm clock, it provides a comprehensive tool to plan your day, build better time habits, and manage your schedules in an intuitive, brain-friendly way.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- ⏰ **Custom Clocks & Alarms** – Create flexible notifications that go beyond standard system alarms.
- 📅 **Day Planning** – Structure your schedule into time blocks and get a clear overview of upcoming tasks.
- 🔄 **Daily Synchronization** – Align your tasks with your natural activity flow to work smarter, not harder.
- 🔔 **Smart Notifications** – Never miss important focus sessions or tasks with reliable reminders.
- 🎨 **Minimalist Design** – Clean, modern UI designed to minimize distractions and keep you focused.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

Developed using **Dart** (and the **Flutter** framework) for multi-platform support, high performance, and smooth animations.
